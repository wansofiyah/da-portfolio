Marketing Campaign Effectiveness: A/B Testing

Individual project · Statistical analysis · Excel

Question
Which of three campaigns performs best for a simulated product launch?
Method
ANOVA to detect differences, then Bonferroni-corrected t-tests to compare campaigns pairwise.
Finding
Campaign 1 or 3 outperforms Campaign 2 with statistical significance.
Lesson
Running many comparisons without correction quietly inflates false positives.
